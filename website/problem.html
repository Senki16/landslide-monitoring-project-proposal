The problem

Colombia pays for landslides after they happen.

Landslides in geologically critical areas threaten the safety, economy and integrity of the people who depend on the road network. Public spending still goes to response instead of prevention.

Why Alto de Pavas.

San Francisco is the municipality with the most landslides in Antioquia (DAGRAN, 1992–2022). The study focus is a curved section with a back slope at Alto de Pavas (5.950459, −74.861388), km 87 towards Medellín, on the San Luis – Cocorná toll section operated by Devimed.

Curves with cut slopes concentrate drainage problems and geological stress. Heavy seasonal rainfall, limestone mining, livestock farming and roadside housing add pressure. In October 2020, landslides in San Luis left 2 dead and 3 injured; on 22 June 2024, a slide fully closed the highway.
