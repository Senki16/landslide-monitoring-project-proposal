The solution

From satellite data to early warnings.

A geographic monitoring platform with Industry 4.0 technology. It builds on NASA's LHASA approach —rainfall plus terrain susceptibility— and adds computer vision on satellite imagery.

Step 1

Ingest

NASA (GPM IMERG, LHASA, Earthdata), SIATA, IDEAM and IGAC feeds go into a geospatial and meteorological database.

Step 2

Assess

A dynamic risk matrix based on R = H × V × E and the ISO 31000 process scores every monitored section.

Step 3

Detect

A YOLO-based neural network looks for scarps, cracks and debris in multispectral imagery.

Step 4

Warn

A web GIS dashboard, bulletins and configurable alerts for DAGRAN, INVIAS and the concession.

The strongest of four alternatives.

Each alternative was scored on Industry 4.0 technologies, innovation, impact on the population and commercial viability. The monitoring platform scored 68 of 75 points.

Six specific objectives.

  1. DatabaseA geospatial and meteorological database that stores and manages data in real time.
  2. Dynamic risk matrixTerrain stability, weather and other variables combined to prioritize prevention.
  3. Neural networkAn algorithm trained to detect critical patterns and predict landslides.
  4. Monitoring platformReal-time visualization, automatic reports and configurable alerts.
  5. Pilot testsIterate with real data to ensure effectiveness and scalability.
  6. ReportsAutomated technical recommendations for road and disaster managers.
