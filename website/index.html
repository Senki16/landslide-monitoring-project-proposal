About this demo. Alert thresholds are illustrative: 40, 70 and 100 mm in 3 days, or 150, 250 and 350 mm in 15 days, raised one level when surface soil wetness exceeds 80%. The full platform would calibrate them with local landslide records, IDEAM and SIATA gauges and the neural-network detections described in the proposal. NASA POWER data usually lag 2–3 days; IMERG shows the latest available day. Sources: NASA GPM IMERG via GIBS, NASA POWER (LaRC), NASA EONET and NASA Worldview imagery. Basemap © Esri.

Monitoring platform assisted by computer vision and neural networks for identifying potential landslides and reducing disaster-prevention costs.
