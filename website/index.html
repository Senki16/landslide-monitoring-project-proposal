About this demo. Alert thresholds are illustrative: 40, 70 and 100 mm in 3 days, or 150, 250 and 350 mm in 15 days, raised one level when surface soil wetness exceeds 0.8. The full platform would calibrate them with local landslide records, IDEAM and SIATA gauges and the neural-network detections described in the proposal. NASA POWER data usually lag 2–3 days; IMERG tiles show the latest available day. Sources: NASA GPM IMERG via GIBS, NASA POWER (LaRC), NASA EONET. Basemap © Esri.
